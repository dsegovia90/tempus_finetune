Clock times, read in your browser

A small model trained to find every clock time in a chat message and say what time of day it means: a finetuned small language model (LFM2.5-230M or Qwen3.5 0.8B), or a tiny non-generative model (BERT-mini) that tags the times in the message and reads each one. It runs on this device; nothing is sent anywhere. Code, data and results on GitHub.
